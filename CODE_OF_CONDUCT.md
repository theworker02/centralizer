# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Centralizer a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainers through a private GitHub Security Advisory or the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will review and respond. Consequences may include a warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban from the project.

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and when an individual is representing the project.
